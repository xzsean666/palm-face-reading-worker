## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Aesthetic Narrative
This design system bridges ancient Eastern esoteric metaphysics (天机推演、易经象数、星象八字) with cutting-edge decentralized intelligence (Web3 Cryptography, Zero-Knowledge proofs, and AI predictive algorithmic engines). The interface evokes reverence, transcendent mystery, and technological certainty. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Mystic Cyber-Occultism**—a fusion of deep midnight celestial indigo, sacred gold leaf gilding, and ethereal neon telemetry. It replaces cold utilitarian fintech crypto-skeuomorphism with ritualistic depth: sleek dark glass, cosmic geometric motifs, micro-gradients of celestial auroras, and radiant sacred geometry accents.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for digital natives, crypto traders, astrology and metaphysics enthusiasts, and Web3 prediction market participants who seek algorithmic insight through classical metaphysical lenses. The UI must evoke:
- **Solemn Intuition:** A sense of communion with ancient wisdom, free from clutter or gaudiness.
- **Cryptographic Trust:** Strict, high-precision typography and crystal-clear transaction states.
- **Sovereignty & Elegance:** Luxurious tactile contrast between deep space blacks and metallic gilded accents.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for high-contrast legibility in deep dark environments, balancing warm metallic gold accents against cold cosmic indigo depths.

### Core Swatches & Token Map
- **Primary Gilded Gold Tier**:
  - `primary` (#D4AF37): Pure radiant gold for primary action buttons, key indicators, critical numerical accents, active icons, and celestial high-focus elements.
  - `primary-light` (#F5D97E): Lustrous soft gold highlight used in gradient stops, badge highlights, and cosmic aura boundaries.
  - `primary-deep` (#9A7B1E): Antique deep gold for gradient anchors, active state presses, and refined gilded borders.
- **Surface & Void Tier**:
  - `background-base` (#0B0E1A): Deep midnight indigo-black, absorbing visual noise and serving as the bottom canvas.
  - `surface-card` (#141828): Mid-indigo card fill, defining container bounds and grouped modules.
  - `surface-elevated` / `surface-input` (#1B2138): Interactive hover surfaces, input fields, dropdown trigger pools, and modal sheets.
- **Mystic & Cyber Accents**:
  - `secondary` (#7B5CFF): Occult violet for AI calculation states, cosmic energy bars, non-fungible divination badges, and metaphysical depth cues.
  - `tertiary` (#4FD8E8): Quantum cyan for real-time cryptographic sync, Web3 network nodes, on-chain confirmations, and telemetry pings.
- **Typography & Content Hierarchy**:
  - `text-primary` (#F3EFE3): Warm parchment white, soft on the eyes, delivering high legibility without harsh white glare.
  - `text-secondary` (#9CA0B5): Muted lavender-gray for labels, secondary subtitles, metadata, and timestamps.
  - `text-tertiary` (#5D6178): Dimmed astral gray for disabled states, hint watermarks, and subtle auxiliary tags.
- **Functional Semantics**:
  - `status-success` (#3ECF8E): Celestial jade green for successful on-chain transactions, bullish alignment, and auspicious outcomes.
  - `status-warning` (#FF5C6C): Cinnabar coral red for error states, market downfalls, caution warnings, and high-risk divinations.

## Typography

The type system balances sacred Chinese calligraphic proportions with futuristic numerical precision.

### Font Family Fallback Chain
- **Chinese & General Body**: `PingFang SC`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `-apple-system`, `sans-serif`. Used for all conversational divination text, hexagram titles, and general prose.
- **Numbers, Data & Telemetry**: `DIN Alternate`, `Space Grotesk`, `JetBrains Mono`, `sans-serif`. Used for probability ratios, crypto token balances, timestamps, astrological degrees, and algorithmic matrices.

### Rules of Usage
- **Strict Hierarchy**: Never apply pure `#FFFFFF`. Use `text-primary` (`#F3EFE3`) for headlines to preserve an antique, parchment-like luminous sheen.
- **Numerical Alignment**: All numerical tables, wallet addresses, and countdown timers must leverage tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) through the `DIN Alternate` / `Space Grotesk` stack.
- **Letter Spacing**: Use `letter-spacing: 0.05em` on all small caps tags, badges, and uppercase label levels to enhance the crisp, esoteric terminal aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

### Mobile Architecture & Viewport Standards
The mobile frame follows a single-column contextual layout bounded between 360px and 430px viewport widths. Horizontal page edge padding is locked to `margin` (16px / 1rem) to maximize screen real estate while protecting touch affordances.

### Layout Principles
- **No Bottom TabBar**: Screen real estate is entirely dedicated to narrative divination streams, live data charts, and oracle scrolls.
- **Unified 56px Master Top Navigation**:
  - Pinned persistently at `height: 56px` with `z-index: 100`.
  - Top safe-area inset integrated (`padding-top: env(safe-area-inset-top)`).
  - Left zone: Drawer trigger (`☰`) or navigational back arrow (`←`) centered in a 40x40px touch footprint.
  - Center zone: Brand monogram or current oracle chamber title.
  - Right zone: Activity bell with notification badge dot, paired adjacent to the gilded quick-action creation button (`+`).
- **Vertical Rhythm**:
  - Component internals: 8px (`space-sm`) and 12px padding.
  - Spacing between related items in a stack: 12px to 16px (`space-md`).
  - Distance between discrete functional card groups: 20px (`space-lg`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use mundane drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **Cosmic Stratification**: luminescence, dark glass transience, and subtle metallic borders.

### Elevation Levels
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base)**: Surface `#0B0E1A`. Void plane with optional ambient radial nebula blurs in `#7B5CFF` (opacity 8%) and `#D4AF37` (opacity 4%).
- **Layer 1 (Cards & Modules)**: Surface `#141828`. Solid grounding layer wrapped with a ultra-fine border: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating / Active Containers)**: Surface `#1B2138`. Outlined with `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`. Emits a faint ambient gilded halo: `0 8px 24px rgba(11, 14, 26, 0.6)`.
- **Layer 3 (Overlays, Bottom Sheets & Top Header)**: 
  - Top Navigation: `#0B0E1A` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a bottom border `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
  - Modals & Drawer: Surface `#141828` with top highlight rim `1px solid rgba(245, 217, 126, 0.3)` and deep cosmic occlusion `0 24px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.

## Shapes

The shape system blends the organic flow of celestial circles with the structured geometries of Taoist bagua and digital chips.

### Component-Specific Corner Radii
- **Action Buttons**: Full capsule shape (`border-radius: 24px` for 48px height, `9999px` for variable pills), signifying complete cosmic unity and seamless gestural tactility.
- **Structural Cards & Panels**: Fixed at `border-radius: 16px`, providing an architectural yet approachable frame.
- **Inputs & Data Fields**: Fixed at `border-radius: 12px`, establishing distinct interior nested harmony when placed inside 16px cards.
- **Micro Tags & Chips**: Fixed at `border-radius: 6px` to 8px for snappy, data-dense tokenized markers.
